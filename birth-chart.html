Get Your Astera Reading

Enter your birth details to discover your zodiac sign and receive your daily, weekly or monthly horoscope.

A full personalised natal chart will be available in a future version of Astera.

A birth chart, also called a natal chart, is based on the date, time and place of birth. It shows the positions of the planets at the time a person was born.



















Your birthplace is used to suggest a matching location through the Open-Meteo Geocoding API. Birth time and email are not currently used. Astera does not save your birth details.
