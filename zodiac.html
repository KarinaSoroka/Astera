Discover the Zodiac Signs

In astrology, the zodiac is divided into twelve signs. Each sign is associated with different characteristics and one of four elements: Fire, Earth, Air or Water.

Explore the twelve zodiac signs below to learn about their traditional meanings and characteristics.

The Twelve Zodiac Signs

Aries

21 March – 19 April

Element: Fire

Aries is traditionally associated with confidence, energy and independence.

Taurus

20 April – 20 May

Element: Earth

Taurus is associated with stability, patience and determination.

Gemini

21 May – 20 June

Element: Air

Gemini is associated with curiosity, communication and adaptability.

Cancer

21 June – 22 July

Element: Water

Cancer is traditionally associated with sensitivity, loyalty and emotional awareness.

Leo

23 July – 22 August

Element: Fire

Leo is associated with creativity, confidence and generosity.

Virgo

23 August – 22 September

Element: Earth

Virgo is associated with practicality, organisation and attention to detail.

Libra

23 September – 22 October

Element: Air

Libra is traditionally associated with balance, fairness and cooperation.

Scorpio

23 October – 21 November

Element: Water

Scorpio is associated with determination, intensity and emotional depth.

Sagittarius

22 November – 21 December

Element: Fire

Sagittarius is associated with optimism, independence and a love of exploration.

Capricorn

22 December – 19 January

Element: Earth

Capricorn is associated with discipline, responsibility and ambition.

Aquarius

20 January – 18 February

Element: Air

Aquarius is traditionally associated with originality, independence and innovation.

Pisces

19 February – 20 March

Element: Water

Pisces is associated with imagination, compassion and intuition.
