About Astera

Welcome to Astera, a website created to explore my interest in astrology.

Astrology has been one of my hobbies for many years. I do not consider astrology a science, but as a highly pragmatic person, I find it an interesting contrast to my everyday way of thinking. Reading and learning about astrology is something I enjoy and find relaxing.

I chose astrology as the theme for this website because it allows me to combine a personal interest with learning something new. Astera is also an opportunity for me to practise and develop my HTML and CSS skills as part of my web development studies.

As my skills develop, I hope to continue improving the website by adding new pages, information and interactive features.
